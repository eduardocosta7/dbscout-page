DBScout Subscription Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This policy explains how DBScout Pro is sold, charged, renewed, cancelled and refunded. It is part of the DBScout Terms of Use and is written to meet U.S. automatic renewal laws (including the Restore Online Shoppers' Confidence Act and state laws such as California's Automatic Renewal Law), while preserving the mandatory rights of consumers in other countries.

Automatic renewal terms, in plain words: DBScout Pro is a paid monthly or yearly subscription. Its price, plus applicable taxes, is shown at checkout before you pay and in the confirmation e-mail. After the free trial shown at checkout (currently 3 days), your card is charged and the subscription renews automatically every month or year at the then-current price until you cancel. You can cancel online at any time, in the customer portal, and keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Payments are non-refundable, except where the law requires otherwise.

1. Plans

Free — no account, no key, no time limit. You can browse every app, database and table on the connected phone or iOS Simulator (macOS), or in a .db file, with the grid, sorting, column filters, paging and live updates, and see the preferences of each app.

Pro — everything in Free, plus:

  • the SQL query tab (reading and writing), with saved queries and query history;
  • cell editing with a double click, in table tabs and in query results;
  • deleting a row with the trash button;
  • editing and deleting app preferences (Android);
  • exporting a table or a query result to CSV, Excel (.xlsx) or TXT;
  • database snapshots: saving a full copy of a database on this computer, restoring it and deleting it;
  • "What changed?": comparing a snapshot with the database as it is now, or two snapshots with each other;
  • the AI assistant chat (in the desktop app: Claude Code, Codex, Gemini, Ollama or a tool you add yourself; in the extension: Claude Code and Codex) and AI access to the databases and app preferences from your editor.

What Pro does not include. The subscription pays only for DBScout. It does not include, and we do not sell, any third-party product or account: the AI assistants require your own account with Anthropic, OpenAI or Google (and any plan or fee they charge), or your own Ollama installation, and adb and Xcode come free from Google and Apple. You are never required to buy anything from a third party to use DBScout, and we receive no commission from them.

2. Prices

DBScout Pro has a monthly plan and a yearly plan. The price of each is the one shown on the checkout page before you confirm the purchase, and it is repeated in the confirmation e-mail. The price you agreed to stays until you are told otherwise as described in section 5. Prices and trial periods may be extended, reduced or replaced by Special Conditions as described in section 4; the amounts and periods stated in this policy are not a commitment to maintain them.

Prices are in the currency shown at checkout (US dollars or your local currency where available). Taxes (such as VAT or sales tax) may be added at checkout depending on where you live. Your bank may charge currency conversion fees.

3. Who sells it

DBScout Pro is sold through Polar (Polar Software, Inc.), which acts as the merchant of record: Polar processes the payment, charges applicable taxes, issues the receipt and sends your license key by e-mail. Polar's terms also apply to the purchase. By completing the checkout you give your affirmative consent to these automatic renewal terms, and Polar sends you an e-mail confirming the subscription, its price, its renewal period and how to cancel.

4. Free trial

  • Both plans start with a free trial. Its length is shown at checkout before you confirm (currently 3 days). A payment card is required at signup.
  • The first charge happens only when the trial ends. Cancel before the trial ends and you are never charged.
  • A key works on one computer at a time. To use it on another computer, remove it from the first one in DBScout (or deactivate it in the customer portal). One computer can use any key you own, one after the other.
  • Special Conditions. The trial length and the prices stated in this policy are those in force on the effective date above and are given for reference. At our sole discretion, and without any obligation to do so, we may make available longer or additional trial periods, promotional prices, discounts, coupons or other special conditions (together, "Special Conditions"), in general or to specific customers, campaigns or channels. Special Conditions are shown at checkout before you confirm the purchase, apply only on the terms and for the period stated there, and, unless they say otherwise, do not alter any other provision of this policy (including automatic renewal, cancellation and refunds). The terms shown at checkout when you confirm the purchase are those that apply to that purchase. A Special Condition granted to one customer creates no right for any other customer, and its expiry or withdrawal does not affect periods already agreed.

5. Renewal

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period (month or year) and the card on file is charged, until you cancel. If we change the price, we will tell you by e-mail before the new price applies to your next renewal; you can cancel before it does.

For yearly subscriptions, a reminder is sent by e-mail before each renewal, with the date, the price and how to cancel.

If a renewal payment fails, Polar may retry it. If it is not paid, the subscription ends and DBScout returns to the free plan.

6. Cancellation

You can cancel online, at any time, without having to contact anyone, in the customer portal (in DBScout: License → Recover it in my account, or the link in your Polar e-mail). After cancellation:

  • you keep Pro until the end of the period already paid;
  • there is no further charge;
  • DBScout then returns to the free plan. Nothing on your computer or phone is deleted.

7. Refunds

The free trial exists so you can try every Pro feature before paying. For that reason, charges are not refundable, including for partial periods, unused time or a cancellation in the middle of a period — except where the law of your country gives you a right to a refund, which this policy does not limit. Refund requests that the law requires are handled through Polar; write to support@dbscout.com.br and we will help.

A charge made in error (for example, charged twice) is always refunded.

Consumers in the EU, UK and similar jurisdictions: by starting the subscription you ask for the digital service to begin immediately, during the withdrawal period, and acknowledge that you lose the right of withdrawal once the service has been fully provided, to the extent your law allows. Where your law still grants a withdrawal or refund right, it applies.

Chargebacks: if you dispute a charge with your bank instead of contacting us, the license key is deactivated while the dispute is open.

8. License key and computers

  • One license key works on one computer at a time. On that computer, the same key covers both the DBScout desktop app and the DBScout extension.
  • To move to another computer, remove the key from the old one (License → Remove from this computer) or deactivate it in the customer portal, then activate it on the new one.
  • DBScout checks the license online about every 6 hours. Without internet, Pro keeps working for up to 72 hours after the last successful check, and then returns to the free plan until it can check again.
  • Sharing, reselling or publishing a key is not allowed (see the License Agreement). A key that is refunded or charged back stops working at the next check. A key whose subscription you cancelled keeps working until the end of the period already paid (section 6), and stops at the first check after that.

9. Changes to Pro

We may add, change or move features between Free and Pro as the product evolves. If a change removes a Pro feature you paid for, it takes effect at your next renewal, and you can cancel before it. Changes are announced in the release notes.
